## Phase 2 Accomplishments

### Track A: Verticality & Floor Elevation
- **Dual-Layer Elevation Sockets**: Extended tile catalog sockets in `src/tileCatalog.js` to support dual-layer elevation (`ground` and `elevated` sockets).
- **Vertical Meta-Tiles**:
  - `Ramp`: Connects `ground` socket on one side to `elevated` socket on opposite side.
  - `Bridge`: Connects `elevated` sockets across canyon gaps at height (`BRIDGE_HEIGHT = 1.5`).
  - `Ladder`: `Ramp` variant authored at a steeper 1-wide footprint.
  - `CANYON_IMPASSABLE`: Extended with `elevated` socket options.
- **Heightmap Floor Elevation & Auto-Step Movement**:
  - `generateHeightmapGrid` in `src/landforms.js` and `applyVerticalBridgeFeature` in `src/verticalWfc.js` assign sloped floor heightmap gradients.
  - `updatePlayer` in `src/threeGame.js` smoothly lerps `player.position.y` toward `getTerrainHeightAt(x, z)` for top-down auto-stepping traversal.

### Track B: Pocket World Overhaul
- **Centralized Simulation Pause Gate**:
  - Wrapped surface-only per-frame update calls (`updateBunkerBlastDoor`, `updateBiomeEnvironment`, `updateScatter`, `updatePickups`, `updateCorpses`, `updateLoreDrops`, `updateBuildSiteBeacon`, `updateConsoles`, `updateLoreTerminals`, etc.) in `if (!this.isInPocket)` inside `render()` in `src/threeGame.js`.
  - Added `updatePocketContent(delta, now)` gated `if (this.isInPocket)` to update pocket content without surface bleed.
  - Skipped companion movement while `isInPocket === true` (companions wait at the surface hole).
- **Uncollectable Pickup Fix**:
  - Registered pocket reward pickups in `this.pickupMeshes` in `mountPocket` in `src/threeGame.js`.
- **WFC 2x2 Pocket Generation**:
  - Rebuilt pocket generation on `collapsePocketLattice` + `stampLattice` on a 2x2 lattice of 7x7 tiles (`POCKET_GRID_SIZE = 13`).
