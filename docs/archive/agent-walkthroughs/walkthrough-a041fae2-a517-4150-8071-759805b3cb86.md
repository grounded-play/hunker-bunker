# Biomechanical Visual & Gameplay Remaster — Walkthrough

We have completed the full visual and gameplay overhaul of Hunker Bunker to align with the dark, atmospheric biomechanical aesthetic of [title_key_art_v2.png](file:///home/caveman/Desktop/icecave/hunker-bunker/public/title_key_art_v2.png).

---

## Key Achievements

### 1. Key Art Visual Aesthetic Alignment
- **Specular Wet Metallic Floor**: Updated floor material roughness (`0.32`) and metalness (`0.65`) to achieve wet metallic floor reflection matching the key art.
- **Volumetric Atmospheric Fog**: Atmospheric mist and dark chitinous lighting tone matching the Gigeresque artwork.
- **Biomechanical Arch Structures**: Doorways and ring road intersections feature Gigeresque ribcage frame arches.

### 2. Multi-Stage Fungal Hole Enemies
- **Stage 1 (Spore Vent)**: Dormant/bloomed bio-luminescent vents nest inside pit holes (`isHoleTile`), lobbing spore pods toward approaching operatives.
- **Stage 2 (Mycelium Stalker Eruption)**: Upon taking damage or when player approaches within 3.2 units, the vent erupts into a fast, aggressive `mycelium_stalker` that lunges with dynamic momentum.
- **Death Effects**: Defeated Mycelium Stalkers explode into lingering toxic spore puddles (`spawnToxicSporePuddle`).

### 3. Expanded Enemy Types & Momentum Physics
- Added **Biomechanical Charger** (`bio_charger`), **Mycelium Stalker** (`mycelium_stalker`), **Spore Mortar** (`spore_mortar`), and **Spore Vent** (`fungal_spore_vent`) into `src/data/enemies.js`.
- Implemented **Momentum & Wall-Sliding Physics** (`updateChargerOrStalkerBehavior`): Enemies accelerate, steer, slide along walls, and lunge at the player.

### 4. Concentric Ring Road System & Interactive Wall Doors
- **Ring Road World Layout**: Generated Inner (radius 10), Mid (radius 24), and Outer (radius 40) Ring Roads connected by radial corridors in [landforms.js](file:///home/caveman/Desktop/icecave/hunker-bunker/src/landforms.js).
- **Biomechanical Blast Doors**: Interactive doors placed at key ring road junctions. Doors have 3 HP (easy to breach by shooting) and can be opened with Interact (`E`).
- **Enemy Door Breaches**: Low-level bugs, snails, and stalkers will attempt to breach closed doors if blocked while pursuing the player.

### 5. Camp Navigation Conduits & Compass Guidance
- **Illuminated Floor Conduits**: Glowing amber cable trails leading along Ring Roads from spawn/bunker directly toward Survivor Camps.
- **HUD Compass Guidance**: Compass calculates nearest undiscovered Survivor Camp and provides directional angle and distance telemetry (`getCampCompassState`).
