# Walkthrough: Player Night Visibility Enhancement

We implemented fog exemption on the player and torso sprite materials and boosted local lighting parameters around the player at night. This resolves the visibility issue where the player silhouette becomes muddy and indistinguishable under heavy night/fog cycles.

## Changes Made

### Sprite Fog Exemption
- **[src/threeGame.js](file:///home/caveman/Desktop/icecave/hunker-bunker/src/threeGame.js)**:
  - Added `fog: false` to the `THREE.SpriteMaterial` options for `this.playerMaterials` (bottom half/legs sprite).
  - Added `fog: false` to the `THREE.SpriteMaterial` options for `this.playerTorsoMaterials` (top half/torso sprite).
  - This ensures that the player's paper-doll billboards are rendered with 100% texture clarity, preventing them from being tinted by dark fog colors (such as the deep green of the BIO sector).

### Local Lighting Boost
- **[src/threeGame.js](file:///home/caveman/Desktop/icecave/hunker-bunker/src/threeGame.js)**:
  - Updated `updateDayNightCycle`:
    - Raised the night-time `playerGlow` point light intensity multiplier from `1.55` to `2.6` and increased its distance from `13.5` to `16.5` units.
    - Raised the night-time `playerLightPool` visual overlay opacity multiplier on the floor from `1.2` to `1.85`.
    - This creates a brighter, wider puddle of light directly beneath and around the player under dusk/night conditions, illuminating the floor and nearby wall meshes.
