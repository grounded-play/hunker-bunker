# Walkthrough - Wall Varieties, Pit Holes, and Enhanced Credits

All changes requested by the user, including subsequent iterations (transparent alpha borders, lower spawn rates, larger scales, falling death physics, and custom bullet impact decals), have been successfully implemented and verified.

## Changes Made

### 1. Visual Wall Varieties & Spawn Tuning (`src/threeGame.js`)
Wall generation in `mountChunk` deterministically handles grid wall tile (`#`) creation with adjusted spawn rates for better balance:
* **Hole / Pit** (6%): A flat plane chasm mesh with randomized scaling from **1.5x up to 4.0x** tile size (providing the requested 4x4 scale variety) and random Z-rotation for organic variation.
* **Hazard Wall** (16%): Normal wall block decorated with a dark cylinder base and a red warning siren light dome. Emits a pulsing red point light.
* **Damaged Wall** (13%): Shorter wall block (45-70% height) with random rotational tilts and scattered physical rubble meshes at the base.
* **Standard Wall** (65%): Normal full-height wall with standard decorations (vents, pipes, brackets, pillars).

### 2. Falling to Death Physics Animation (`src/threeGame.js`)
* **Collision Bypass**: Modified `canOccupyPosition` to skip check-overlaps on active hole center tiles. This allows players (and knockback effects) to slide onto hole tiles instead of treating them as solid obstacles.
* **Float-based Collision Circle (`isPlayerOverAnyHole`)**: Added a helper method to calculate if the player's exact float coordinates fall inside any nearby scaled hole's physical radius. The physical fall radius is calculated dynamically based on each hole's randomized scale (e.g. `scale * 0.42`), making the physical and visual boundaries align perfectly.
* **Fall Mechanics**: Added a state checker in `updatePlayer` that triggers when the player steps inside a hole's radius. It locks inputs, plays a deep structural warning rumble sound, and initiates a falling sequence:
  * Sinks down on the Y axis (`position.y -= 3.5 * delta`).
  * Spins rapidly around the Y axis (`rotation.y += 8.0 * delta`).
  * Shrinks uniformly to 0 (`scale` goes to 0).
* **Defeat Event**: Once the player falls to `y <= -2.5`, it executes `takeDamage(999, 'abyss')`, logging a suite failure to the permanent death archive (black box) under the cause "abyss".
* **Reset Lifecycle**: Added a clean reset in `respawnPlayer` to restore the player group scale, rotation, and falling flags to normal on a restart.

### 4. Custom Bullet Impact Decals (`src/threeGame.js`)
* **Texture Generation**: Generated a borderless circular sci-fi molten impact crater sprite (`bullet_impact_1780686391976.png`) to replace the initial square-framed version.
* **Alpha Keying**: Wrote and executed a Python flood-fill script (`/scratch/process_scars.py`) to transparentize the black background borders (making 523,945 pixels transparent) and output to [decal_scars.png](file:///home/caveman/Desktop/icecave/hunker-bunker/public/decal_scars.png).
* **Decal Mesh & Shader Material**: Replaced the basic material inside `spawnWallDecal` with a custom `ShaderMaterial` utilizing a specialized WebGL fragment shader.
* **Dynamic Cooling & Desaturation**: The custom shader takes a `uCooling` uniform that transitions from `0.0` (hot, molten glowing orange) to `1.0` (cooled, charred black-and-white soot mark) over the first 1.5 seconds of the decal's life. The decal's overall opacity then fades smoothly to zero over its 5.0 second duration, creating an incredibly realistic cooling effect. Decals are placed flush against hit wall coordinates, facing the collision normal.

### 6. Front-End Asset Preloading (`main.js` & `src/audio.js`)
* **Image Preloading**: Added `/pit_hole.png` and `/decal_scars.png` to the main `gameplayManifest.images` array in `main.js`. This guarantees that they are preloaded and cached by the WebGL/Three.js asset loaders before the player drops into the sector, preventing any runtime loading hiccups or pop-in.
* **Audio Preloading**: Verified that all new action sounds (such as `weapon_reload`, `weapon_fire_sidearm`, `enemy_hit_soft`, `enemy_death_crawler`, `enemy_death_snail`, and `amb_metal_stress`) are already mapped and loaded dynamically by `AudioManager.loadAssets` in the `gameplayManifest.audio` registry at startup.

### 7. Banjo-Kazooie Dialogue Sound Style (`src/dialogue.js`)
* **Trigger Cadence**: Changed the typing sound trigger frequency from playing on every single character (which sounded too robotic/buzz-like) to playing only on word boundaries (space character) or randomly on other characters with a `22%` probability.
* **Vibrant Pitch Variation**: Applied a cartoonish, wide-ranging random playback rate (ranging from `0.55` to `1.5`) on each triggered sound. This results in a classic "talking" cadenced vocal blip sequence, perfectly capturing the whimsical retro speech style of Banjo-Kazooie.

### 8. Custom Death Cause for Falling (`main.js`)
* **Abyss Cause Mapping**: Added the `'abyss'` death reason mapping to `causeMap` in `generateDeathReport`. This displays `> CAUSE: EXOSUIT GRAVITATIONAL FAILURE — PLUMMETED INTO PIT CHASM` on the game-over screen when the player falls to their death in a chasm, instead of showing the default generic suit failure log.

### 9. Seamless Gameplay Transition (`main.js` & `style.css`)
* **HUD Hidden Class**: Added a `body.hud-hidden` selector group to `style.css` to hide all HUD elements (e.g. health, O2, touch controls) independently of the 3D scene.
* **Refactored Transition Timing**: Updated `runMissionIntroSequence` in `main.js` to manage the transition stages:
  - When the user confirms the dialogue, we add the `hud-hidden` class and trigger the door transition.
  - When the doors are fully closed (`onClosed` callback), we remove `mission-intro-active` which reveals the 3D scene behind the closed doors without any pop-in.
  - Once the doors slide open, we wait `1000ms` for the transition to finish before removing `hud-hidden`, letting the HUD fade in smoothly after the gameplay view is fully visible.

### 10. Chunk Mount Performance Optimizations (`src/threeGame.js`)
* **Eliminated Dynamic Allocations**: Replaced the dynamic creation of geometries and materials on every chunk load (which triggered severe Garbage Collection stutters and GPU upload frame drops).
  - Pre-allocated static geometries (`sirenBaseGeometry`, `sirenDomeGeometry`, `rubbleGeometry`) and basic materials (`sirenBaseMaterial`, `sirenDomeMaterial`) in the constructor to be shared globally.
  - scaled unit meshes (`rubbleGeometry` scaled to randomized sizes) and the existing static `wallGeometry` (Y-scaled for randomized damaged wall heights), removing 100% of runtime layout allocations and geometry creation.
  - Added proper resource disposal (`dispose()`) call overrides for all new shared objects to avoid memory leaks.
