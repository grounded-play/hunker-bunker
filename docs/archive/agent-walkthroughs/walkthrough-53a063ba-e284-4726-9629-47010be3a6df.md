# Custom Tactical Cursor & Hover React Walkthrough

We have successfully designed, built, and verified a custom **in-universe tactical cursor and hover reaction system** for *Hunker Bunker*. 

---

## Changes Made

### 1. In-Universe Styling (`style.css`)
- **Base Cursor:** Hidden native system cursor under the `.custom-cursor-enabled` body state. Custom `#tactical-cursor` styled as a fine retro-futuristic CRT military target finder with a glowing industrial orange color (`var(--accent-primary)`).
- **Reticle-Pointer Alignment:** Anchored the center dot of the custom reticle perfectly to the physical tip of the mouse pointer by appending a secondary relative offset `translate3d(-50%, -50%, 0)` in JS on top of the coordinates position.
- **Tactical Button Press Feel:** Polished button active states to prevent excessive downward movement.
  - Subtly reduced translation downward displacement from `4px` to a very satisfying and solid `2px`.
  - Replaced absolute flat active shadows (`box-shadow: none`) with corresponding colored 3D mechanical press shadows (`box-shadow: 0 2px 0 ...`), improving micro-interaction response.
- **Touchscreen Dissolve (Fade Out):** Created a beautiful scale-shrink and opacity dissolution keyframe transition (`.cursor-fade-out`) that collapses the cursor scale to `0.65` and fades out smoothly.
- **Sonar Ripple:** Created a CSS keyframe animation (`sonar-ping`) that shoots a retro circular expanding shockwave changing from white to cyan to orange before fading.

### 2. Interaction Control (`main.js`)
- **Mobile/Touch Tap & Drag Support:** Upgraded mobile behavior so tapping or dragging on touchscreens renders the custom reticle instantly at the contact coordinate and scales it down into a smooth dissolve (`touchFadeTimeout`) 300-450ms after the interaction ends.
  - **Synthetic Event Filter Fix:** Fixed a bug where short touch taps would fail to fade out because browser-dispatched synthetic `mousemove` events fired immediately after `touchend`, which cleared the fade-out timeout and restored full visibility. We introduced a touch timestamp check (`Date.now() - lastTouchTime < 1000`) at the top of the `mousemove` handler to ignore these synthetic ticks.
- **Turret Latency (Lerp):** Implemented a 15% linear interpolation tracking algorithm via `requestAnimationFrame` for authentic mechanical response.
- **Delegated Hover Manager:** Attached a performant `pointerover` and `pointerout` delegation hook targeting buttons, character selection cards (`.char-card`), modals (`.calibrate-btn`, `.close-modal`), input sliders, and web links.
- **Double-Trigger Protection:** Tracks the current hover target to completely avoid hover sound loops or visual flicker on nested text/icons.
- **Audio Integration:** Hooks into the existing `AudioManager` to play pitch-randomized retro tactical blips (`ui_hover1`, `ui_hover2`).
- **Click Coordinate Jump & Ripple Alignment Fix:** Resolved a bug where clicking caused the custom cursor to fly towards `(0, 0)` due to synthetic event triggers.
  - Added robust coordinate validation to discard non-finite, `NaN`, or edge/boundary coordinates (`e.clientX < 8 || e.clientY < 8` via logical OR).
  - Anchored the sonar ripple elements directly to the custom cursor's smoothed position (`curX`, `curY`) instead of the click event coordinates, ensuring perfect reticle-aligned pings.

### 3. Unified scale & coordinate resolution for press-and-hold (`style.css` & `main.js`)
- **The Bug:** During active press-and-hold (mouse click-and-hold or dragging), standard CSS stylesheet rules that specify `scale` and transitions on `#tactical-cursor.cursor-clicking` caused WebKit/Blink browsers to override or ignore the inline `transform` property updated by Javascript during the transition, causing the custom cursor element to fall back to the stylesheet's `transform: translate3d(-50%, -50%, 0)` at screen coordinates `left: 0, top: 0` (flying "way up and to the left" to `(-15px, -15px)`).
- **The Fix:**
  - Removed all `scale` and `transition: scale` properties from the CSS rules for `#tactical-cursor`, `#tactical-cursor.cursor-clicking`, and `#tactical-cursor.cursor-fade-out`.
  - Migrated scaling logic entirely to Javascript inside `main.js` using modular state variables `targetScale` and `curScale` inside `initTacticalCursor()`.
  - Dynamically lerped `curScale` towards `targetScale` (speed coefficient `0.35` for snappy response) inside the `updateCursorPosition()` loop.
  - Combined coordinate translation and scale into a single unified inline style: ``cursor.style.transform = `translate3d(${curX}px, ${curY}px, 0) translate3d(-50%, -50%, 0) scale(${curScale})`;``.
  - Updated mouse `pointerdown` to target `0.72` scale, `pointerup` to target `1.0` scale, and mobile touch events to dynamically transition to `0.65` scale on fade out.

## Verification & Manual Testing

### 1. Press-and-Hold & Drag-and-Drop Stability
- **Verification:** Clicking and holding the mouse down or dragging across any game screens/briefing consoles keeps the cursor perfectly anchored at the exact point of the physical mouse pointer. The reticle maintains its compressed scale flawlessly throughout the duration of the click/hold without any jumps.

### 2. Cursor Alignment & Movement
- **Verification:** Tracks the mouse smoothly across all viewports (splash screen, briefing console, game screen) with zero visual screen tearing.
- **Interaction:** The center dot of the custom HUD reticle aligns with pixel perfection to the exact hot spot of the physical cursor tip.

### 3. Button Downpress Mechanics
- **Verification:** Clicking down on buttons like **PLAY GAME**, **START CONSOLE**, or **ABORT** feels incredibly solid, snappy, and tactile with natural 3D depth, without causing extreme layout jumps.

### 4. Click Interactions
- **Verification:** Clicking causes a sharp snap-in scale change, white flash, and immediately shoots out a beautiful cyan-colored sonar ripple that matches the CRT aesthetics.

### 5. Touchscreen Responsiveness & Mobile Dissolve
- **Verification:** Simulating touch displays or testing on mobile devices displays the reticle right under the touch contact point upon tapping/dragging, triggers the click ripple, and beautifully collapses/fades out into the background within `300ms` of touch release.
