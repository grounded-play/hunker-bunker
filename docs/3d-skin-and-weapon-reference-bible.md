## 1. Modeling T-Pose Reference Standards

* **Pose Geometry**: True neutral 3D modeling T-Pose — arms held straight horizontally at 90° from torso, palms facing downward, fingers relaxed and slightly spaced, feet shoulder-width apart pointing forward, spine straight, head aligned with horizon.
* **Camera Setup**: Straight-on front orthographic view with pure black background (`#000000`) for clean silhouette and alpha transparency extraction.
* **Lighting**: 3-point studio key/fill lighting with sharp edge rim lighting to highlight biomechanical bevels, ribbed tubing, and chitinous specular gloss.
* **Rigging Compatibility**: Rooted at `mixamorig:Hips` matching `Scout.game.glb`, `tank-rigged.glb`, and `engineer-rigged-gestures.glb`.

## 3. 3D Modeling Pipeline Checklist

1. **Model Import**: Load base mesh (`Scout.game.glb`, `tank-rigged.glb`, `engineer-rigged-gestures.glb`) into Blender / Maya.
2. **Sculpting / Retopology**: Match the T-Pose proportion, aligning the ribbed tubing, spinal vertebrae, and chitinous armor plates to the base armature.
3. **PBR Shading**:
   - `Base Color`: Deep carbon, bone white, olive drab, or dark teal.
   - `Roughness`: `0.15 - 0.35` for wet chitin/glossy domes; `0.6 - 0.8` for matte carbon mesh.
   - `Metallic`: `0.7 - 0.9` for industrial plates; `0.0 - 0.2` for organic chitin.
   - `Emissive`: Cyan (`#00f0ff`), Amber (`#ff9f1c`), or Emerald (`#34d399`) for vascular conduits and ocular visors.
4. **GLTF Export**: Export as binary `.glb`, run through `gltf-transform optimize --meshopt`, and save into `public/3d/runtime/new3ds/`.
