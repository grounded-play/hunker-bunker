# Log 14 Audit — Armory, Navigation, and Runtime Clues

## Confirmed from `docs/logs/log14.json`

- The session exercised chassis, weapon archetype, weapon finish, charms, and both overclock bays.
- The finish control was technically present, but there was no persistent central readout confirming the selected sheen.
- The session ended in the Armory, making hidden-screen cleanup important to verify.
- Menu renderer state still showed 131 draw calls, 499 geometries, and 205 textures after gameplay.
- Gameplay climbed to roughly 1.1 GB estimated GPU memory, 4,279 wall instances, 1,116 draw calls, 1.27M triangles, and 759 transient effects.
- Adaptive quality engaged at approximately 5.8 FPS and disabled post-processing/shadows.
- Initial chunk mounting produced repeated 84–104 ms long tasks; this is a streaming hitch, not a shader compile failure.

## Changes made

- Armory now disposes its renderer, GLB scene graph, textures, and animation loop on every exit, including return to the menu.
- Armory now exposes an explicit `WEAPON SHEEN / TACTICAL FINISH` selection and central live-preview readout.
- Operator sheen/tint selection is reachable from Armory through the existing Suit Tint Matrix.
- Hero selection now has an explicit `RETURN TO MAIN MENU` control with the normal bulkhead transition.
- Debug `GIVE MONEY` also healed the operator; with Queen's Milk equipped that
  heal became backlash damage (`queens-milk-backlash`) and could create a death
  while a menu preview was open. Debug grants now bypass relic backlash, and
  non-gameplay previews reject damage defensively.
- The black-box marker event stored coordinates under `detail.state`, while the
  objective listener read `detail.x`/`detail.z`. The objective now receives the
  saved coordinates, and defeating the corrupted operator explicitly retitles
  the objective to `RECOVER BLACK BOX — GUARD DEFEATED` and points the player to
  the next step.
- Full-screen stage and modal sizing now resolves against `#game-viewport`,
  avoiding browser `vw`/`vh` overflow when the 16:10 game stage is letterboxed.

## Follow-up performance risks

- The 1.1 GB gameplay peak remains a separate world-streaming problem: wall-instance growth, transient-effect accumulation, and texture retention need profiling next.
- The 84–104 ms chunk-mount tasks should be budgeted into smaller mount batches.
- The log contains no clear JavaScript exception or failed asset request; the strongest actionable clues were lifecycle retention and world/render growth.
