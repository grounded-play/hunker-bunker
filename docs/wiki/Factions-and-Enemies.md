# Factions and Enemies

## The Survivor Camps

Three distinct survivor camps exist within the active sector network. They serve as trading partners, quest givers, and potentially allies—or targets.

### 1. Meridian Camp (The Tech-Scavengers)
* **Leader**: Overseer Kaelen (Engineer)
* **Identity**: Former engineers, maintenance crews, and salvage operators. They are tech-worshippers who believe the bunker's central computer is a sleeping god.
* **Economy**: High demand for raw shells and energy cells; surplus of computer parts and technology modules.
* **Aesthetic**: Sparking capacitors, cable bundles hanging like vines, cyan diagnostic screens.

### 2. Tallow Camp (The Hydro-Cultists)
* **Leader**: Sister Martha (Scout)
* **Identity**: Agrarian outcasts who cultivate mutated bioluminescent flora in the hot steam shafts. They are deeply spiritual and pacifist.
* **Economy**: High demand for metal plates and medical tech; surplus of bio-vaccines and synthetic rations.
* **Aesthetic**: Thick steam mist, orange heat lamps, metal pipes wrapped in moss and bio-spores.

### 3. Vesper Camp (The Iron Guild)
* **Leader**: Commander Briggs (Tank)
* **Identity**: Horizon Corp's former security guards and mercenary contractors. Highly militarized, suspicious of outsiders, and heavily armed.
* **Economy**: High demand for medical supplies and rations; surplus of ammunition and weapon upgrades.
* **Aesthetic**: Sandbag barricades, heavy automated turrets, red alert strobes, and steel blast doors.

## The Alien Presence: The PregAlien Hive

Deep within the core reactor room lies a biomechanical organism of unknown origin. 

* **The Queen**: A colossal, telepathic bio-mechanical matriarch. She views human survivors as raw biomass for her brood or as organic hosts. She communicates via direct neural static on the Tactical Console.
* **The Amber Eggs**: Translucent, pulsing clutches glowing with warm bio-luminescence. Inside them, embryos fuse with corporate technology.
* **The Green Infection**: Sickly bioluminescent green veins that crawl over metal bulkheads, converting standard computer terminals into organic "mimics" and humans into cyber-hybrids.
