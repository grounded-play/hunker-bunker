# Locations

## Horizon Corporation's Subterranean Bunker Network

The game takes place across multiple sectors of this massive underground complex on Cocytus IV.

### Sector Zero: "The Cave"
The core reactor room of the facility. It is now the epicenter of the alien infestation and the resting place of **The Queen**. The deepest, most dangerous part of the bunker.

### Sector A-9: Meridian Camp
The ruins of the high-voltage power grid. Now occupied by the tech-worshipping Meridian Camp. The area is filled with sparking capacitors and hanging cable bundles.

### Sector B-4: Tallow Camp
Geothermal steam vents that once powered the facility. The Tallow Camp uses this heat to cultivate mutated flora. The air is thick with steam and lit by orange heat lamps.

### Sector C-7: Vesper Camp
A high-security armory vault. Vesper Camp has heavily militarized the zone with sandbag barricades, automated turrets, and red alert strobes.

---

*Note: Exploring beyond these known sectors requires careful management of your Exosuit's oxygen supply and defense systems.*
