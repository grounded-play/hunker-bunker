# System Breakdown: Factions, Camps, and Hives

## Product Role

Camps and hives are the consequence engine. They make a procedural run remember how the player acquired safety, resources, and passage. Their value comes from repeated verbs and visible aftermath—not the number of internal meters.

## Human Camps

- **Meridian / Kaelen:** systems and grid identity; engineering affinity and radar/power support.
- **Tallow / Martha:** biological survival and care; health/humanity-facing identity.
- **Vesper / Briggs:** security, ammunition, and fortification.

Camps support investment, status changes, quests, class relationships, active faction verbs, worker behavior, and audio cues. The original “verbs still need to be built” framing is stale: camp economy activation and ambient human AI are connected and tested. Human acceptance remains needed for whether they feel distinct and understandable.

## Hive Sites

- **Suture / Nahl:** masking, healing, resin, and bodily compromise.
- **Relay / Vey:** secrecy, network interference, and forged-clearance possibilities.
- **Carapace / Rhun:** defense, living armor, and hull identity.

Each hive has persistent status, extraction history, bond, quest flags, and boarding implications. Act 1 treatment affects Act 2 disposition. The PM should protect the reveal: early interactions must be interpretable as resource decisions but retrospectively legible as harm or relationship.

## State Versus Presentation

Internal state includes humanity, infection load/stage, cover integrity, suspicion, Queen obedience, camp status/bond/passenger state, network propagation, and hive status/bond. Most of it should remain backstage.

Player-facing surfaces should answer:

1. What does this faction currently think of me?
2. What changed because of my last action?
3. What option did I gain or lose?
4. What might happen at boarding?

Examples such as `TRUSTED / FORTIFIED` are good summary language, but must link to a reason or recent event when ambiguity matters.

## Visible Aftermath

The largest open quality gap is physical and social aftermath. A fortified, robbed, culled, turned, or outed camp should not share the same population, props, audio, and interaction affordances. Sprint 22 should select a minimal but unmistakable aftermath matrix rather than add more hidden variables.

## Acceptance

- Play one supportive, exploitative, and hostile route.
- Verify workers and leaders reflect state changes.
- Confirm active verbs have distinct utility and feedback.
- Verify suspicion/network changes have understandable causes.
- Verify hive wounds and bonds persist across save/reload/death.
- Confirm manifest previews match actual eligible passengers.

## Non-Goals Without a New Decision

Full escort simulation, deep population economies, PvP/co-op faction play, and unrestricted real-money faction items are not implied by the current system.
