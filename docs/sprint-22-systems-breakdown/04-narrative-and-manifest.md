# System Breakdown: Narrative Structure and Boarding Manifest

## Narrative Promise

The first act teaches survival logic; the second act reveals that survival actions were social and biological choices. The four-seat vessel forces the player to turn relationships into a final, painful manifest.

## Implemented Spine

- Director Chen and the 0047 logs establish the containment conspiracy.
- A unified Specimen 0047 codex entry unlocks only after the relevant evidence is found and identifies the dormant Queen lineage.
- Class-keyed wreckage logs pay off tracking/relay/weapon ship identities.
- Camp leaders and the Queen react to death.
- Act 2 provides Queen/system beats, camp/hive consequences, launch readiness, manifest preview, and ending explanations.
- Ten ending families and cutscene identifiers are declared and tested.

The older scope item “build the mathematical solver” is complete. The remaining question is whether players discover and understand it.

## Manifest Actors

- player (always consumes the base seat);
- recruited or turned human passengers;
- rescued hive allies;
- Queen status;
- egg status and stability;
- knowledge/outing and infection state, which influence ending interpretation.

The manifest validates capacity and story blockers before departure. The UI can forecast a likely ending, but copy should preserve uncertainty where hidden information is part of the drama.

## Ten Ending Families

Original: Full Brood, Clean Escape, Mixed Crew, Carrier's Bargain, Scorched Sky.

Expanded: Mothership Infection, Alien Exodus, Outed Escape, Failed Carrier, Empty Husk.

Not every ending needs equal beta prominence, but every shipped path needs a tested trigger, available media/fallback, and causal explanation.
