# Hunker Bunker — Art Style Bible: "Nordic Cathedral Biomech"

> Owner direction, 2026-09-25:
> - **Art Nouveau / Jugendstil meets Giger: sensual cathedrals of deep, dead corporate
>   space gods**, injected into every part of the game.
> - Refined the same day: **Giger meets Northern Scandinavian design, specifically
>   Nordic Jugendstil / National Romanticism.** Not literal: transposed, then
>   **decayed**, and then **biology takes form within it**. The subject is the
>   juxtaposition of that hard-line beauty with organic form, and the synthesis they
>   make, in a beautiful dark decay.
>
> This is the one style every art prompt, painter brief and UI skin starts from. It
> supersedes the store's "Shared style block" in
> `docs/steam-store-placeholder-assets-and-prompts.md` (§ Shared style block, below,
> is its replacement).

## 1. The idea in one paragraph

A dead megacorporation built its bunkers as **cathedrals to its gods**: the space
entities it worshipped, sold, and was swallowed by. Its engineers drew from **Northern
Scandinavian Jugendstil and National Romanticism**: austere, hardline craftsmanship,
weathered granite geometry, blackened iron bindings, and cold bone inlays. Every pipe
is a taut Nordic curve, every hatch an arched portal, every terminal a reliquary.
Then the gods' biology grew from within the architecture. The ribs of the nave are
vertebrae; the organ pipes breathe; biological forms take shape inside the metalwork.
The mood is **the juxtaposition of hardline northern beauty vs. organic biomechanical form**:
a synthesis of **decoratively dark decay** where ancient craftsmanship and rot become one.

**The three ingredients, always together:**

| Ingredient | What it contributes | Look to |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Northern Scandinavian Jugendstil / National Romanticism** | the **hardline beauty & line**: austere monumental Nordic geometry, restrained taut curves, blackened iron bindings, bone and stone relief, frost-etched leaded glass, arched portals, disciplined balance; transposed into decaying industrial hardware | Nordic National Romanticism (Saarinen's Helsinki station, Sonck's granite churches), stave-church carving and structure (abstracted), Nordic granite and iron craft |
| **Biomechanical surrealism** | the **internal biology**: forms taking shape *from within* the chassis; ribbed conduits, vertebral struts, wet chitinous carapaces, bio-sac gauges; claustrophobic curvature pushing through cold metal seams | airbrushed biomechanical surrealism, spine-fused architecture, surgical and deep-sea machinery |
| **Corporate cathedral** | the **meaning & sacred decay**: nave ribs, vaulted arches, sun-disc windows, altarpieces, reliquaries, votive lamps; corporate scripture plaques, dead executives as saints, decoratively dark decay | Gothic/Nordic cathedrals, corporate brutalism, reliquary metalwork, frost-etched bronze |

### Three states, always readable in any image

| State | What you see | Where it dominates |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Order**: the Nordic design | hard-line monumental geometry: stepped granite massing, deep arched portals, blackened-iron strap bindings, carved bone and stone relief, restrained taut curves, disciplined proportion | the ship, the Mothership, fresh sectors, the operator's gear, menus |
| **Decay**: the design, abandoned | frost-etched and verdigris bronze, rust-bled iron bindings, cracked and spalled granite, lichen and mineral bloom, soot, failing lanterns | most of the bunker; everything the player explores |
| **Synthesis**: biology inside the design | biomechanical forms growing **from within**, through joints, bindings and cracks, then **adopting the design**: vertebrae that follow the arch, carved interlace becoming living tendrils, membranes filling the portals, bioluminescence where lanterns were | hives, the Queen, deep sectors, and the player as infection rises |

The drama lives at the **boundary**: a perfect granite arch split by a rib; an iron
binding that has become a tendon; a carved interlace you realise is breathing.
**Every growth starts at a joint, seam or crack**, never from a clean face.

**What already fits:** `public/title_key_art_v2.png`, the biomechanical ribbed arch
over a lone operator, bone, brass and fog, is the closest existing piece. The door art
(`public/door_*_keyart_v2.webp`) has the right density and construction but needs
the curves.

## 2. Form language

- **Hard-line first.** Monumental, rectilinear massing (stepped blocks, vertical
  ribs, deep reveals) with a few **restrained, taut curves**. Beauty comes from
  proportion and craft, not from ornament everywhere. This is National Romantic
  restraint, not French whiplash excess.
- **Arched portals.** Round and parabolic arches with heavy keystones frame doors,
  panels and portrait windows. Pure rectangles are reserved for the glass that live
  numbers sit behind.
- **Blackened-iron bindings.** Strap hinges, banding, rivets and ring clasps hold
  everything together; they are where decay bleeds and where biology gets in.
- **Carved interlace.** Tight serpentine interlace carving in stone, bone and timber
  (the stave-church tradition, **abstracted: no literal runes, no Viking cliché**).
  It is the bridge motif: carved lines that, in Synthesis, become living tendrils.
- **Sun-discs and iron rings** for anything round and radial: the radar, door locks,
  a portrait's backing disc, the Mothership's eye.
- **Frost-etched glass** in small leaded panes; cracks, frost and blood follow the
  lead lines.
- **Ribs and vertebrae** as the biology's structure. In Synthesis they line up with the
  arches and stepped blocks.
- **Sensual curvature** belongs to the biology: bodily, suggestive form, the swell of
  a rib, a membrane stretched over a portal, in the biomechanical-surrealist tradition. It is carried
  entirely by form and surface. **No nudity, no sexual acts, no genitals,** so every
  asset stays Steam-store-safe.
- **Corporate liturgy:** the dead corporation's glyph-plaques and seals carved like
  monument inscriptions (invented glyphs, never real script), safety codes cut in
  stone, founders as carved saints in niches.

## 4. Rendering

**Carved line over painted biomech:**
- heavy black contour on silhouettes;
- a **woodcut / etching** line for stone, iron and carving: cut, disciplined, directional
  hatching (the Nordic print tradition);
- looser, organic hatching with wet highlights for the biology, so the two line
  qualities carry the juxtaposition themselves;
- soft cel shading with a **wet sheen** on bone and chrome;
- painted grime and rust.

No photoreal materials, lens effects or depth of field in 2D assets.

Light is **lantern-cold**: amber lanterns low and inside (lamps, seams, windows), a
pale northern cold light on stone faces, teal for screens, bioluminescence where the
biology rules, and deep black above. There is always one strong focal glow.

## 5. Shared style block (prepend to every art prompt)

```
"Nordic Cathedral Biomech" style for the game "Hunker Bunker": the hard-line beauty of
Nordic Jugendstil / National Romantic architecture — monumental stepped granite
massing, deep round arches with heavy keystones, blackened-iron strap bindings and ring
clasps, carved bone and stone relief, abstract serpentine interlace carving, frost-
etched leaded glass, restrained taut curves — transposed into the sacred bunkers of a
dead megacorporation's space-god cult and DECAYED: spalled granite, rust-bled iron,
verdigris bronze, lichen and soot, failing lanterns. From WITHIN that design, an
internal biomechanical biology takes form: ribs, vertebrae, tendons, tubes and wet membranes grow
out of joints, bindings and cracks, and deeper in adopt the design — vertebrae following
the arches, carved interlace becoming living tendrils. Sensual organic curvature carried
by form only (no nudity). Beautiful, dark, decorative decay; the juxtaposition of
hard-line craft and organic form is the subject. 2D illustration: heavy black contours,
woodcut/etching hatching on stone and iron, looser organic hatching with wet highlights
on the biology, soft cel shading with painted texture. Mostly darkness; amber lantern
light, pale cold light on stone, teal only for screens, bioluminescent green only on
the biology. No text unless specified.
```

**Avoid (paste into negative prompts):** readable text, logos with real brands,
watermarks, photorealism, 3D render look, lens flare, depth of field, nudity, genitals,
sexual acts, gore beyond blood splatter, clean sterile sci-fi, flat vector minimalism,
French Art Nouveau excess (Mucha florals, whiplash everywhere), Viking clichés (horned
helmets, longships), literal runes or real scripts, gold-leaf opulence, extra hues
outside the palette.

## 8. Prop prompt audit: stop making the subtext literal

The production prompt catalogs were reviewed together, including the 3D master
backlog, the October gap/60/80-prop batches, world-completion and lore TSVs,
Sprint 34 prop prompts, the placeholder catalog, cave assets and the lived-in
cutscene set. They share a useful material vocabulary, but overuse the same ribs,
wet membranes, openings and bodily comparisons. That repetition makes the intent
blunt, makes unrelated props look interchangeable, and weakens the few moments
that should feel intimate or transgressive.

The fix is **implication by design**, not a lighter tone. Hunker Bunker remains an
adult horror game. Unease comes from forced proximity, ritual, pressure, scale,
sound, rhythm and tactile material contrast. Do not name sexual anatomy or make a
functional prop into a one-note anatomical joke. The player should notice the
second reading after first believing the object could work.

### The one-organic-idea budget

Every ordinary prop gets one primary organic idea. Hero props may get two when the
ideas serve different functions. Everything else stays industrial and specific.

| First read | Allowed organic cue | Keep industrial |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| valve / pump | a pressure pulse beneath one gasket | wheel, flange, bolts, service access |
| terminal | a cable bundle that tightens when used | screen hood, keys, vents, mounting |
| bed / cradle | fitted segmented cushioning | restraint hardware, hinges, drainage, frame |
| door | tendon-like tension only at the opening seam | slab mass, tracks, lock, emergency release |
| pipe / conduit | one vertebral support rhythm | diameter, elbows, clamps, insulation, flow path |
| altar / relic | preserved tissue behind glass | plinth, reliquary case, fasteners, offerings |

Do not automatically add ribs, tendrils, wet sheen, bone, pores and membranes to
the same object. Reserve a full stack for hive landmarks and ending imagery.

### The three-pass prop prompt

Write prompts in this order:

1. **Gameplay truth:** exact noun, use, dimensions, support plane, silhouette,
   moving parts and player-readable state.
2. **World truth:** maker/faction, repair history, wear caused by use, and one
   story clue. Damage must have a direction and cause.
3. **Style dose:** one Nordic construction motif, one organic intrusion emerging
   from a believable seam, the local light/accent, then the render contract.

If removing the style sentence makes the asset unidentifiable, the prompt is not
specific enough. If removing the noun still leaves the usual “wet ribbed shrine,”
the prompt is too style-heavy.

### Specificity and variation locks

- Give every prop a **dominant primitive**: tall cylinder, low wedge, arched slab,
  offset tripod, hanging crescent, or stacked rectangular mass. Do not default to a
  centred vertical shrine.
- Choose a **construction owner**: corporate original, field repair, camp salvage,
  hive conversion, or cryo adaptation. Never blend all five.
- Name a **wear story**: hand-polished handle, heat-blued exhaust side, mineral
  runoff below a failed seal, boot impacts at one corner. “Grimy/weathered” alone
  is not art direction.
- Use **asymmetry with cause**: replacement hinge, shifted load, missing cover,
  growth following heat. Random asymmetry reads as generation noise.
- Use only one emissive family per ordinary prop. Teal is diagnostic, amber is
  human practical light, green is living biology, red is immediate danger, violet
  belongs to the Queen/cosmic corruption.
- Literal icons, words and decals are authored after generation. Ask the model for
  blank plates and clean decal zones.

### Prop prompt template

```text
[ASSET CONTRACT: isolated 3D game prop, GLB-ready, scale and support plane]
[FUNCTION + SILHOUETTE: what it does, dominant primitive, readable moving parts]
[CONSTRUCTION OWNER: materials, fastening method, repair history]
[STATE STORY: one caused wear pattern and one gameplay-readable state]
[STYLE DOSE: one Nordic structural motif; one organic cue emerging only from a seam]
[PALETTE/LIGHT: base materials plus one emissive family]
[CAPTURE: orthographic-friendly three-quarter view, neutral grey, full object visible]
[EXCLUDE: unrelated anatomy, stacked motifs, people, text, scene dressing, pedestal]
```

Example:

```text
Isolated GLB-ready emergency coolant bypass, 1.1 m tall, wall-backed with a flat
mounting plane. An offset iron cylinder feeds two clearly connected pipes through a
large manual wheel and a glass pressure chamber. Corporate original with one camp-
welded replacement bracket; the lower-left seam is mineral-stained from a persistent
leak and the wheel rim is polished by gloves. A restrained carved-bone arch protects
the gauge; beneath one failed flange, a single living pressure sac contracts against
the bolts. Soot-black iron, bone ivory and one dim cyan diagnostic. Neutral 18% grey,
three-quarter product view, entire silhouette and connections visible. No words,
people, floor scene, loose tendrils, generalized rib cage, literal anatomy or pedestal.
```

## 9. Mature-content direction

Hunker Bunker is an **Adults Only** title on Steam (owner, 2026-10-27). Every
cutscene is **the grindhouse cut**: explicit sex and schlock violence, Troma
midnight-movie excess fused with Giger's biomechanical eroticism. Stills and key art
outside cutscenes keep sensuality through form and surface (sections 2 and 5).

The grindhouse cut has hard edges that every prompt, still and clip keeps:

- **Adults only, unmistakably.** Every person on screen is a consenting adult over
  twenty-five, and prompts say so with an age. The negative prompt always carries
  `child, minor, teenager, youthful face, school uniform`.
- **Consent.** Sex is always mutual and willing. Nobody asleep, unconscious, captive
  or coerced is ever the subject of a sexual act; sleep films show the couple before
  they sleep.
- **Gore is aimed at the monsters.** Crawlers, mutants, drones and husks are blown
  apart, boiled, crushed and shredded, as camp and wet as possible. Violence is never
  done to a sexual partner and is never sexual.
- **The operator stays faceless.** The player's helmet stays sealed (identity lock,
  section 10); armour may be unlatched or stripped.

Use locally controlled models and record the model, LoRAs, checkpoint hash, workflow
version, seed and references for every approved asset (the pipeline writes them
beside every take). Before shipping, the in-game Content Guide
(`src/matureContentAudit.js`) and the Steamworks content survey must describe the
explicit sex and graphic violence the cutscenes show.

## 10. Cinematic clip locks

Every cutscene job has three authored anchors:

| Anchor | Must define | Must not change |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| First frame | cast, pose, camera height/lens, screen direction, set geography, key light | class armour identity and subject count |
| Action | one camera verb, one primary subject verb, restrained secondary motion, state transition | costume, architecture, palette, object inventory |
| Last frame | resolved pose, changed prop/light state, final composition and hold | identity, screen direction, set geography |

The action is a transition, not a second still prompt. Use timed clauses in order:
“0–2 s: camera pushes; 2–5 s: operator lowers the lever; 5–7 s: conduit light
settles; final 12 frames: hold.” Avoid simultaneous action lists.

### Player-character identity lock

When the player is visible, attach the canonical class identity PNG from
`art/source/art-remaster/sprite-v4/<class>/identity/`. Describe only stable,
observable attributes already in that reference. Repeat this lock in both endpoint
stills and the motion prompt:

```text
IDENTITY LOCK: preserve helmet silhouette, visor shape and colour, armour
proportions, plate layout, material colours, knee lights, tools and body scale from
the reference. Helmet stays sealed. Do not expose a face, swap class parts, recolour
the suit or alter the number of operators.
```

For co-op, number references and assign screen position before the action begins.
Never ask the motion model to invent a player appearance from the word “operator.”

### Style and continuity lock

The cutscene style lock (the source of truth is `scripts/cinematics/cut-prompts.json`):

```text
STYLE LOCK: Hunker Bunker grindhouse cut, explicit adults-only sci-fi schlock in the
spirit of a Troma midnight movie fused with H.R. Giger biomechanical eroticism:
glistening black latex, ivory vertebrae, ribbed sexual biomechanical architecture of
phallic pistons and vulvic portals, deep-space corporate industrial horror. Lurid
practical light, amber and acid green, wet reflections, splattered gore, grindhouse
35mm grain and print scratches; every person a consenting adult over twenty-five.

CONTINUITY LOCK: preserve subject count, screen direction, set geometry, costume
damage, prop placement, palette and key-light direction from the approved first
frame. Only the described action changes state. No new objects or characters.
```

Negative prompts name drift failures and the age floor, not merely quality
adjectives: `deformed anatomy, duplicated subject, identity drift, morphing face,
melting geometry, rubber motion, flicker, camera jump, text, watermark, child, minor,
teenager, youthful face, school uniform`.

### The grindhouse cut: anchors and models

Each cut's first frame, last frame and action are explicit: a sex act between
consenting adults (with the motion LoRA's trigger: `m15510n4ry`, `c0wg1rl`,
`d0gg1e`, `bl0wj0b`) and a gore beat against a monster, timed so both read in five
seconds.

| Anchor | Model | Add-ons |

| First frame, key-art cuts | Z-Image Turbo (redrawn from the scene brief) | Giger style + photoreal nudity LoRAs |

## 11. Approval gates

A generated prop is not approved until it passes silhouette at thumbnail size,
support-plane/scale, function, motif-budget, palette and text-free checks. A clip is
not approved until the first and last frames match their anchors, the character
survives a six-frame contact sheet without class drift, architecture stays rigid,
and the final composition holds long enough to cut cleanly.

The executable clip workflow and commands live in
[`docs/cinematics-comfyui-pipeline.md`](../cinematics-comfyui-pipeline.md).
