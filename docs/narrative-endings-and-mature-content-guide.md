## 1. Executive Narrative Philosophy

*Hunker Bunker* is a psychological sci-fi survival roguelite where the core dramatic spine pivots around **infection, moral complicity, identity loss, and self-sacrifice**:

> *"Everything you build in Act 1 becomes leverage, hostage, or weapon in Act 2."*

When the operative descends into the depths of Sector Zero and becomes infected by the Queen, the game confronts the player with severe psychological choices:
- **Do you surrender your human will to the Queen**, allowing your mind to be crushed and enslaved as her obedient carrier?
- **Do you choose self-annihilation (suicide/self-sacrifice in the void)** to ensure the alien pathogen never reaches human civilization?
- **Do you make a tragic bargain**, silently hiding the parasite in your own flesh while helping human survivors escape?
- **Or do you betray human civilization entirely**, smuggling the infection onto the Mothership?

## 2. Deep Dive: Story Choices & Mature Narrative Dilemmas

### Choice Category A: Self-Annihilation / Suicide in the Void (Dying to Protect Humanity)

In multiple narrative branches, the operative realizes they are carrying an extinction-level biological weapon. To protect the rest of humanity from contagion, the player can choose ultimate self-isolation or self-destruction:

#### 1. Ending: `EMPTY_HUSK` (Choosing to Perish Alone in the Freezing Dark)
* **Narrative Context**: The operative launches the escape vessel with no passengers, no allies, and no eggs—choosing to drift alone into the freezing vacuum with life support running down.
* **Player Motivation**: Refusing to become a vector of infection for either human colonies or the Queen. Choosing total isolation over biological betrayal.
* **In-Game System Dialogue**:
  ```text
  SYSTEM: LAUNCH COMPLETE. CREW MANIFEST: ONE.
  SYSTEM: THE CAMPS ARE SILENT. THE HIVES ARE SILENT. THE QUEEN IS SILENT.
  SYSTEM: THERE WAS SO MUCH TO CARRY. YOU CARRIED NOTHING.
  ```
* **Cutscene / Visual Art**: [ending-emptyhusk-poster.jpg](../public/cutscenes/ending-scorchedsky-poster.jpg) — A solitary vessel drifting into the dark void with flickering cabin lights.

#### 2. Ending: `SCORCHED_SKY` (Nihilistic Self-Sacrifice / Purging All Biological Life)
* **Narrative Context**: The player purges every egg clutch, destroys every bio-node, and incinerates the survivor camps before launching into space alone with zero trajectory or rescue beacon.
* **In-Game Dialogue**:
  ```text
  SYSTEM: Launch complete. Crew manifest: one.
  SYSTEM: No beacons behind. No signal ahead.
  SYSTEM: Four seats. One heartbeat.
  ```
* **Cutscene Asset**: [ending-scorchedsky.webm](../public/cutscenes/ending-scorchedsky.webm).

#### 3. Log C11 & Courier Arc: Pvt. M. Reyes' Sacrifice
* **Narrative Context**: Pvt. Reyes' hidden letter in the bunker ruins acknowledges her inevitable death and acceptance of self-sacrifice:
  ```text
  LOG C11: "To my sister: I don't think I'm making it out of here. 
  The Mothership knows what's happening. They always know. 
  Don't let them tell you it was an accident. — Pvt. M. Reyes"
  ```
* **Outcome**: Delivering this letter to Commander Briggs triggers the memorial dialogue: *"Reyes wrote this? She always said she would make it home on paper if not in person."*

#### 4. Log C13: Director Chen's Sealed Confession ("You Are The Containment")
* **Narrative Context**: Director Chen reveals that all operators sent down into Sector 9 were never meant to be rescued: their mission was a suicidal containment lock to keep the Queen trapped beneath the ice.

### Choice Category B: Subjugation & Mind Crush (Obeying the Queen Until Your Will Breaks)

#### 1. Ending: `FULL_BROOD` (Complete Surrender to the Parasite)
* **Narrative Context**: The player complies with every telepathic command from the Queen. Step-by-step, the Queen overrides the player's personal agency, neural synapses, and memories, turning the operator into a docile living incubator for her brood.
* **The Will-Crush Dialogue Arc**:
  - *Initial Symbiosis*: `"QUEEN: TWO HEARTBEATS. ONE PURPOSE. NOW WE SHARE THE BODY."`
  - *Eliminating Human Ties*: `"QUEEN: THE BUILDERS CANNOT BOARD. CULL THEM, TURN THEM, OR DEFY ME AND HIDE THEM."`
  - *Final Mind Dominance*:
    ```text
    SYSTEM: LAUNCH COMPLETE. CARGO: ONE QUEEN. ONE CLUTCH. NO PASSENGERS.
    QUEEN: PERFECT OBEDIENCE. THE CORE WORLDS ARE WARM AND CROWDED.
    QUEEN: SLEEP, CARRIER. WE HAVE SO MANY WORLDS TO MEET.
    ```
* **Cutscene Asset**: [ending-fullbrood.webm](../public/cutscenes/ending-fullbrood.webm).

#### 2. Ending: `CARRIER'S BARGAIN` (The Hidden Infection)
* **Narrative Context**: The operative rescues the human survivors and boards the vessel, but chooses not to tell them that the alien parasite has already burrowed into their spine. The player knows they will lose their mind during the journey, but chose to save their comrades first.
* **Dialogue**:
  ```text
  SYSTEM: Launch complete. Coolant cell temperature anomaly logged.
  SYSTEM: Operator vitals nominal. Subdermal readings... flagged.
  SYSTEM: The survivors are safe. Nobody checked your neck.
  ```
* **Cutscene Asset**: [ending-carriersbargain.webm](../public/cutscenes/ending-carriersbargain.webm).

#### 3. Ending: `MOTHERSHIP_INFECTION` (Bio-Trojan Infiltration)
* **Narrative Context**: The player cooperates with the Queen to masquerade as an uninfected rescue shuttle, docking with the fleet flagship while carrying dormant parasite spores into the heart of humanity's fleet.
* **Dialogue**:
  ```text
  SYSTEM: MOTHERSHIP CLEARANCE ACCEPTED. SURVIVOR RESCUE FLIGHT LOGGED.
  SYSTEM: THREE PASSENGERS SLEEPING. VITALS CLEAN. YOURS READ CLEAN TOO.
  QUEEN: GO QUIETLY, CARRIER. THE BIG SHIP IS WARM, AND WE ARE PATIENT.
  ```

## 4. Mature Content Verification & Reviewer Fast-Access Guide

For Steam certification and developer testing, all narrative branches, mature cutscenes, and sensitive themes can be verified instantly using the built-in **Narrative & Mature Content Review Suite**:

### Keyboard & Controller Shortcuts
* **Keyboard**: Press **`F9`** or **`Ctrl + M`** at the Title Menu or in-game.
* **Gamepad / Controller**: Press **`LB + RB + Right Stick Click (R3)`** simultaneously.
* **In-Game Terminal / Dev Console**: Press **`F1`** → type `open_mature_gallery` or `play_ending <ending_key>`.

### Direct Verification Commands:

```javascript
// Instant Ending Previews in Dev Console (F1):
window.act2Dev.playEnding('full_brood');          // Preview Queen Will-Crush Ending
window.act2Dev.playEnding('empty_husk');          // Preview Suicide / Cold Isolation Ending
```
