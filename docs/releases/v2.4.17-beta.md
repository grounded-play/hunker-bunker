# Hunker Bunker v2.4.17-beta

Branch: `dev/sprint-52` · 2026-10-15 · 110 commits since `v2.4.16-beta` (`920f2406`)

Sprint 52: **a base you rebuild by hand, a world you can read, and fixes from the 2026-10-12 and 2026-10-14 Deck and PC playtests.**

- **Rebuild the ship by carrying it.** Ship modules, cots and cargo are physical two-handed loads. They slow you, can be dropped when you are staggered, and snap into glowing install sockets. The first night needs a sealed berth: recover walls and doors, carry the cot in, and sleep.
- **Field weapons and fire.** Guns you pick up in a run are real objects you carry and drop. The M-70 flamethrower sweeps a cone, sets enemies burning and melts ice gates, which opens an alternate thermal route.
- **A world that reads as lived in.** 40 pass-2 world-story GLBs, 60 world-expansion models and the flamethrower/rebuild cohort, 12 photoreal PBR surface suites and 48 keyed decals. All are placed by dressing kits and theme catalogs, lit, interactive and destructible.
- **Sound.** Room tone beds, indoor and outdoor acoustics, point emitters, surface impacts, 3D casing Foley and an OST that rotates instead of looping.
- **The HUD moves to a lower console.** A readable lower third, a circular named minimap, colour-coded pickup counters, chat in multiplayer only, and a weapon panel that names the gun in hand.
- **Fixes from the playtests:**
  - guns face away from the operator and sit in the hand;
  - Armory picks survive the launch;
  - doors between rooms drop into the floor;
  - the crash floor blends without seams or sparkle;
  - the crash exit is walled;
  - menus share one spatial navigation model for keys, WASD and pads.

## 3. Weapons and combat

- **Field weapons** (`29ad2639`, `619b666c`): run-only state and a physical runtime. Picked-up guns drop and carry as objects.
- **Flamethrower** (`e510ef1b`, `3486b9f0`, `993e2b91`): a swept cone profile, a burning status, meltable ice gates with a live cache behind them, and its GLB cohort. An alternate-route thermal gate (`db801373`).
- **Guns in hand and in the Armory** (`c1cfca67`):
  - Per-model frames turn the 16 finish models and the Talon-C that were authored muzzle-first on −X, and put each grip in the palm.
  - The Armory close-up and the hero show the same model.
  - Finishes with their own model are no longer painted over.
  - Sheens tint by hue at half strength instead of erasing paintwork.
- **Hero presentation** (`60007a19`, `5e69ac4f`): the equipped armed loadout, a reactive charm, finishes preserved, ready aim.
- **Armory picks survive launch** (`822a27cf`): a run launch called `loadout.reset()` and dropped every Armory choice; it now clears only the fabricated weapon.

## 4. World

- **Doors** (`dffbc224`, `80113a0e`, `68c5a8df`, `6046ed3b`): biome door families with clear thresholds. Every door between rooms now drops straight into the floor; families differ by frame and material.
- **Crash site** (`7e783ad1`, `75c4cdb9`, `a950c20b`, `91a09c38`): a wide two-zone approach. The floor and the crash exit are restored and walled. Kit pieces can no longer be walked through. Pickups and bloom are calmer.
- **Floor** (`0e48c330`): every surface blends at an N-way junction; specular sparkle is tamed (a minimum roughness, normal-variance specular anti-aliasing, distance fade of normal detail).
- **Walls** (`8331043e`, `d5e1ba9f`, `52b1f0ec`): semantic wall runs with instanced facades; wall machines need support.
- **Traversal** (`8035f4aa`, `bc3f5b30`): fragile ledges collapse in stages; exterior spaces vary.
- **Coverage** (`5f887783`, `baffc199`, `f16a8eaf`, `40006f5f`): doorways and recent props are covered, authored rooms and hallways are guaranteed exposure, and expansion dressing stays destructible.
