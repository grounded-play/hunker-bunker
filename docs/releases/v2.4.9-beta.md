# Hunker Bunker v2.4.9-beta

Branch: `dev/sprint-43` · 2026-09-22

Sprint 43: the campaign day cycle becomes playable end to end — fatigue with consequences you carry between runs, a bed that ends the day, a night that changes the world while you sleep — plus the first real attack on "every run is the same map", two progression-blocking world bugs, and Steam Deck pointer input.

**Gates:** `npm test` 3,820 tests across 417 files (100% green) · `npx eslint .` clean · gate-variety ratchet asserts every ring gate keeps ≥8 distinct placements across 60 seeds.

## 1. The Campaign Day Cycle, Playable

The pieces existed and were disconnected: sleeping was nearly unreachable, the sky knew nothing about the day counter, and nothing the night did was visible.

- **Fatigue ladder** (`src/fatigue.js`) keyed to **expeditions since sleep**, not seconds elapsed inside a run. A within-run stamina bar resets every run, so "never slept" could never happen. `RESTED → ALERT → STRAINED → RAGGED → THE LONG DARK`, where every stage past baseline pays for its penalty with an upside (salvage, relic rarity, detection) — pushing on is a gamble, not self-harm.
- **Scars that do not fully heal.** Sleeping from `RAGGED` or worse leaves a mark that lasts the playthrough. A camp medic walks severity down one tier and **floors at 1** — a treated scar is quieter, never absent. `BLUNTED` is untreatable and acquired last, so the end state reads as a campaign run into the ground rather than bad luck on night one.
- **The bed.** A cot in the Sector 0 hab plus the existing camp bedrolls, all asking one shared rule (`canRestNow()`). `SLEEP UNTIL DAY N` previously carried an inline gate that also demanded an Act 2 `dormant` camp, which is why it almost never appeared in play.
- **The clocks are joined.** `timeOfDay` was a 150-second visual sky loop with no relationship to `dayState.day`; a night's sleep left the sky wherever it happened to be. Waking now pins dawn and recomputes sky and lighting immediately.
- **Overnight simulation** (`src/overnightSim.js`) is deterministic, with no RNG: a player must be able to read a camp before resting and know what the night will cost. Hive creep spreads at most one ring per sleep; camps walk one visible step along `secure → strained → breached → overrun`, and are abandoned only after three consecutive neglected nights.
- **The night is visible.** Creep stamps deterministic decal patches that survive reload without doubling; camps lose their dressing in the order a real place would — defences, then stores, then the fire, then the people.
- **Presentation bounded by design rule §2.4**: no dropped inputs, and no effect that removes agency by another route. Strain is a corner-weighted tint that never reaches the middle of the frame, never goes fully opaque and never blurs. Sprint is priced (more O₂, less bonus), never disabled.

## 2. Expedition Variety: Ring Gates Read the Seed

The complaint was "every time I start the game it's the same layout". Measured across 60 seeds before the change:

| Gate | Distinct placements (before) | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `ring-1-gate` | 2 | **10** |
| `ring-2-gate` | 2 | **13** |
| `ring-3-gate` | **1** — always `2,7` | **18** |
| `ring-4-gate` | 3 | **31** |

- **Root cause**: blocker placement in `mazeExpedition.js` was a deterministic argmin over spine chunks — sort by distance from a fixed radius, take `[0]` — with **no `random()` call at all**, while the reservation placer beside it jittered its angle and radius. The seed was real; it never reached the four decisions a player navigates by.
- **Fix**: a seeded pick from the band of spine chunks between the rings a gate separates, tuned against plan validity rather than taste. A wider band ate the route chunks the territory planner needs and produced invalid plans on 5 of 100 seeds; `0.25` with no floor holds 100/100 valid.
- **Hidden invariant made explicit**: a gate needs a spine neighbour that is *orthogonally* adjacent, because the far-side door is named from the delta between two chunks and a diagonal has no cardinal name. The old argmin satisfied this by luck.
- **Variety ratchet** (`src/mazeExpedition.gateVariety.test.js`) fails if any gate drops below 8 distinct placements across 60 seeds, so this cannot silently regress.

## 3. Two Progression Blockers

- **Camp Meridian was invisible.** `getTerrainHeightAt()` returns the same value for two situations it cannot distinguish: the ground really is at ground level, or the chunk has not streamed in yet. Camps are revealed the moment the story says so — after the first boss, from across the map — which is exactly when their chunk is not loaded, so the camp anchored to the fallback height and nothing ever re-anchored it. A camp on raised terrain stayed buried: listed in objectives, invisible in the world. `sampleTerrainHeight()` now reports whether a sample was real, and unanchored camps re-anchor once their terrain arrives.
- **A ring gate could be unopenable.** Setpiece claims legitimately cover crossing chunks — the collapsed bridge *is* the ring-1 gate — but only the claim's pivot chunk produces a room. A gate landing on a non-pivot module accepted the setpiece, which stamps no door and no `gate_control`: the chunk looked right and that ring could never be opened. Crossings now keep an authored room even when sockets do not match perfectly, recorded as `crossingBestEffort` so the compromise is visible rather than silent.

## 4. Steam Deck Input, Armory Progression & Menu Navigation

- **Pointer input and mouse selection**: The UI left controller mode only on `keydown` and `pointerdown`. A Deck trackpad **moves** the pointer without ever clicking. Furthermore, `isSteamControllerInputActive()` unconditionally evaluated to true when on Deck with controllers present, causing pointer movement (`pointermove`) to trigger `ensureControllerMenuFocus()` and snap focus back to the first button on every move while leaving the `controller-mode` focus ring active. Fixed by making `steamInputState.lastInputMode === 'controller'` authoritative for active controller mode, ensuring mouse movement clears controller focus snapping, and preventing MutationObserver from hijacking focus during mouse interaction.
- **Armory advance and back navigation**: In `style.css`, `#game-viewport > #menu.hidden + #start-game` and `#game-viewport > #menu:not(.hidden) + #start-game` used the adjacent sibling combinator `+`. Because `.menu-corner-settings` sits between `#menu` and `#start-game`, the rule never matched, leaving `#start-game` ("ENTER ARMORY", `z-index: 8001`) visible and layered directly over the Armory's footer advance button `#armory-btn-embark` (`z-index: 8000`). Clicking or confirming advance in the Armory re-triggered `openArmoryGate()` instead of progressing to the `#multiplayer-modal` (Solo / Co-Op / PvP). Replaced `+ #start-game` with `~ #start-game` and `~ .menu-corner-settings`, allowing clean progression to game type selection and enabling `#armory-btn-back` to return to the briefing console.
- **Menu controller mapping & spatial focus**: Added native `role="button"` and `tabindex="0"` to `.char-card` in `index.html`. Included `.char-card`, `#start-game`, and the settings gear in `getVisibleControllerFocusables('menu')` and introduced `isElementInFocusRoot(root, element)` so focus boundaries recognize `#start-game` and `.menu-corner-settings` as part of `#menu`, eliminating the focus reset loop on controller navigation.

## Known Open Items

- **Content variety is still flat.** Arrangement now varies; the reservation role inventory and the setpiece list are still **1 distinct across 60 seeds** — the same kinds of places in the same counts every run. This is the next thing a player notices.
- **Creep is cosmetic.** It marks territory but does not yet block movement, damage, or change spawns.
- **Camp condition does not feed the economy.** A breached camp looks breached but trade prices and verb availability are unchanged.
- **`defeatedMilestoneBosses` stores goal keys while ring crossings match on milestone ids**, so that set cannot be passed as `defeatedMilestoneIds`. A crossing can currently learn about a defeat only through the lifecycle.
