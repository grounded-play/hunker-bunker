# Hunker Bunker v2.4.10-beta

Branch: `dev/sprint-45` · 2026-09-23

Sprint 45: every deployment of a campaign now plays differently while the campaign's geography and story stay put. Seeded expedition conditions, camps and hives as six-room compounds, per-campaign gate challenges, and choices that physically change the world — plus a radar/map pass, HUD overlap fixes and a Steam Deck title-screen fix.

**Gates:** `npm test` 3,939 tests across 427 files (100% green) · `npx eslint .` clean · `npm run i18n:audit` unchanged against baseline (0 unlocalized runtime strings, 7 locales at parity).

**Evidence scope.** Every number above is repository automation plus headless-browser checks on a Linux dev machine (desktop Chrome with SwiftShader). Nothing in this release was verified on physical Steam Deck hardware, in a packaged build, in two-account co-op or across a Steam Cloud round trip. The September 22 playthrough log ([analysis](../reports/session-log-analysis-2026-09-23-steam-deck-playthrough.md)) was captured on **v2.4.8-beta, Windows, keyboard and mouse, solo** and cannot certify this build, the Deck or co-op.

## 1. Seeded Expeditions Inside a Persistent Campaign

- A campaign keeps one world seed for its life (`src/campaignWorld.js`); each deployment derives its own expedition seed and rolls one of five conditions (`src/expeditionSystem.js`): Glacial Gale, Spore Bloom, Bio-Resin Surge, Geothermal Arc, Sub-Zero Stillness.
- Conditions are gameplay, not scenery: enemy density, speed and elite odds; salvage payout (fractional multipliers pay as a chance of one more unit); player speed, O₂ drain, cryo hold and shield surge via the existing `loadoutMods` bus; a per-condition kill signature (frost rings, spore salvage, arc discharge) and resin-harvest yield.
- Each condition tints fog and ambient light (colour and intensity only — no light is added, so no material recompiles); Geothermal Arc browns the grid out on a deterministic flicker.
- **Corridor rubble**: each deployment drops 2×2 rubble into ~22% of generic hallway chunks. A pile is only accepted if every walkable region of the chunk stays connected; rooms, doors, anchors, the crash site, the tutorial ring, reserved rooms and crossings are never touched.
- A briefing card on deploy names the condition, its effects, threat, salvage rate and bounty (7 locales).

## 2. Camps and Hives as Six-Room Compounds

- All 12 beats (6 per camp, 6 per hive) have their own fixture sets from the registered prop catalog, with three layout variants each. Site signature props never appear at another site. Every variant and rotation passes floor-connectivity validation.
- A title card names the room on entry (`MERIDIAN COMPOUND · WORKSHOP BAY`), with a NEW tag on first visit.
- Hive rooms carry their own encounter profiles: the defended nest always holds 2–3 spore snails and 1–2 crawlers once its ring is open (previously the generic 0–2 cybersnail profile, so often empty).

## 3. Routes Differ Between Campaigns — Old Saves Keep Theirs

- Route generation is versioned (`ROUTE_LAYOUT_VERSION` in `src/mazeExpedition.js`). New campaigns use generation 2: seed-mixed streams and a per-campaign spine coil (winding direction, turns, wobble). Generation 1 is byte-identical to the previous generator and remains the default, so every pre-existing save keeps the geography its walls, map and doors were recorded against.
- Found while doing this: raw xorshift's first draw is near zero for small seeds, so under generation 1 seeds below ~1000 all shared one site phase (Camp Meridian landed in the same quadrant for seeds 1–24).

## 4. Per-Campaign Gate Challenges

- Gate types stay fixed landmarks (the collapsed bridge is always ring 2). What changes per campaign is how each gate is held: **elite warden**, **collapsed approach** (rubble every deployment), **infested approach** (1.7× spawns) or **blackout** (the approach runs dark). The four are dealt across the four gates without repeats, deterministically from the world seed (`src/gateChallenges.js`), and end when the crossing opens. A radio call announces each once per run.

## 5. Choices That Change the World

- **Canyon bridge**: opening the ring 2 crossing re-tags the corridor beyond the gantry as bridge tile and lays a steel deck with rails and beacons along exactly that corridor.
- **Fortified camps**: the perimeter room holds the camp's turrets and two sweeping searchlights (unlit meshes). The support prompt reads `FORTIFY PERIMETER` on the purchase that raises the grid, and the Act 2 camp modal offers the same choice.
- **Hive outcomes**: a bonded hive grows bio-flora, speeds the carrier on infested ground and opens a bio-conduit in its escape room back to the crash site; a harvested hive sprouts thorn pods, enrages nearby hostiles and drops the rarest overclock the carrier lacks.
- Outcomes persist in the campaign save (`worldTransformations`) and are announced over the radio. Fixed-seed and multiplayer runs keep them live-only.

## 6. Radar and Tactical Map

- A scan now updates the minimap and tactical map immediately (the cached map was never invalidated, so scans only appeared after the next step onto a new cell). Unscanned space is drawn as fog, scanned walls outline corridors, and the pulse animates across the map with a reveal flash.
- A scan reveals only the rooms it reaches; room-less chunks no longer reveal all their floor because one corner was scanned. Authored rooms are now discovered when walked through.
- Connected pit, canyon and cliff tiles are marked as one lip-outlined zone instead of a ring per tile (one outline where the previous code spawned 808 rings in a live test).

## 7. HUD and Presentation

- Loot panel is pinned below the bunker-level panel however many run-modifier chips it shows; `src/styles/expeditionHud.css` pinned it at `top: 132px !important`, which beat every other fix. The mission/prompt column sits below the actual bottom of the notification deck.
- The aim reticle hides over HUD panels (it rested where the deploy click left the pointer — on RADAR SCAN) and never draws at the viewport origin.
- Native pointer, tactical cursor and reticle are hidden during movies, still cinematics, the crash cutscene, RGB cinematics and door transitions.
- **Steam Deck title screen**: the frosted menu veil was a `backdrop-filter` that did not render on the Deck. It is now a blurred copy of the key art (`.splash-frost`) with an ordinary filter blur, which renders regardless of backdrop-filter support. Needs physical-Deck confirmation.

## 8. Crossings: One Gate Identity, Proven Walkable

A valid world plan was never evidence a player could walk through a gate, so `src/crossingNavigation.test.js` now builds each gate's stamped chunk across 12 seeds on both route generations and walks the tile grid. It found five real defects, all fixed:

- **Wrong gate dressed as the bridge.** The valley-bridge setpiece was `rings: [1]`; the canonical collapsed bridge (and its `restore_canyon_crossing` mission and bridge traversal) is ring 2. The setpiece is now keyed to the `collapsed_bridge` feature.
- **Dead-end gate rooms.** The gate room had only straight n/s sockets; when the route turned inside the gate chunk no rotation fit, and the best-effort fallback left one opening unconnected — a sealed room the route bypassed on the ledges. Gate rooms now carry flank sockets.
- **Locked door facing the ship.** When the spine's next point was diagonal, far-side selection fell back to the previous point and stamped the locked crossing door on the inner side. The far side now follows the route away from the ship.
- **Unreachable gates.** The ring lock is a radial boundary, but routes are not radial: where the route dips inward to a gate, the boundary stopped the carrier short of it (seed 3's ring-2 gate could never be opened). The lock is now route-aware — exact for gates every route passes through, radial plus the spine's approach corridor otherwise — and a blocked step returns to the last legal position.
- **Unworkable spur gates.** Generation 1 can place a gate on a spur (208 of 240 generation-1 gates are not route cut points); the spur's single door was locked with the mission console behind it. Spur doors stay open; generation 2 only places gates on cut points (0 of 240 non-cut).

Defeated milestone bosses stored by goal key are now mapped to milestone IDs for crossings, so a recorded defeat opens its crossing even when an older save's lifecycle state lacks it.

## 9. The Night Has Consequences

`src/overnightConsequences.js`: hive creep slows the carrier, thickens O₂ drain and breeds hostiles in the chunks it reaches, and burns at full spread (a bonded hive's creep costs nothing). Camp condition prices help (strained ×1.25 → overrun ×2), a breached camp has no medic and no safe bed, an overrun camp withholds its signature verb, and **SHORE UP DEFENCES** walks a damaged camp one step back for shells — offered first when a player returns.

## Known Gaps (carried into the next milestone)

- **Objective variety**: `ringManifest.js` still builds the same primary / alternative-resource / fallback routes for each ship goal. Conditions and gate challenges change *how* a route plays, not *which* mission it is.
- **Generation-1 gates**: existing campaigns keep their geography, so most of their gates are not route cut points; the ring boundary, not the door, is their lock.
- **Acceptance**: a physical Deck route, two-account packaged co-op, a two-machine Steam Cloud round trip, packaged frame pacing and a new-player Proof Run all remain open.
