# Narrative Script

This is the canonical narrative spine, condensed from the rough screenplay.
Moment-to-moment interaction text belongs in a future content-data file.

## Visual grammar

Black, white, and industrial gray dominate. Digital red belongs to scanners,
warnings, balances, and corporate decisions. Color is withheld until an ending:
violent amber for destruction, muted violet for mutual aid, or no new color for
the system loop.

At first there is no score—only breath, trucks, plastic, conveyors, and
electrical hum.

## Chapter 1: The Descent

Late afternoon. A windowless logistics warehouse sits beyond a desert parking
lot. Elias Morales, a worn warehouse technician in his forties, sits in a
rusted sedan holding an empty albuterol bottle.

The refill is ready: **$286.40**. His balance is **$19.12**. His employee portal
says benefits remain active until 11:59 PM.

Lucia’s message:

> Hi Dad. Mom says don’t forget the purple one. The blue one tastes bad and
> makes my hands shaky. I drew Robot 4A, but I gave him shoes because he looks
> cold.

Her drawing shows a smiling robot arm in sneakers beside a figure labeled DAD.
Elias folds it into his calibration notebook.

Marisol passes on the way in.

> **MARISOL:** You look like hell, Eli.  
> **ELIAS:** That’s my good side.

His scratched badge says TEMP CONTRACTOR. The reader flashes red: ACCESS
GRANTED.

## Chapter 2: Not Harder

Robot 4A is a titanium sorting arm worth $4.8 million. Its terminal identifies
Elias at $16.50 per hour. It crushes an irregular box.

Elias guides the chassis two inches left, presses lightly at the joint, and
taps twice.

> **ELIAS:** Not harder. Smarter.

The claw releases, recenters, and sorts cleanly. Elias records:

> DOUBLE TAP = RELEASE PRESSURE / RECENTER

For a moment the red sensor crosses Elias’s face like recognition. Then work
continues.

## Chapter 3: Neutral Language

A taped box jams the belt. Productivity variance begins counting. Elias clears
it, and 4A breaks its programmed path. The arm strikes his shoulder and temple.

4A receives priority maintenance. Elias receives an incident review.

In a white office, the HR representative shows footage beginning at the point
of impact.

> **HR:** “Point of contact” is neutral until review is complete.  
> **ELIAS:** You got a neutral word for bleeding?

The compulsory swab reads INCONCLUSIVE. HR says that means Elias cannot be
cleared. At 6:42 PM—hours before the stated cutoff—his phone reports:

> COVERAGE TERMINATED  
> STATUS: SEPARATION PENDING REVIEW

## Chapter 4: Three Inches

At an automated pharmacy kiosk, Elias scans the bottle. The machine confirms
the refill and terminated insurance. His final pay is $14 after deductions for
productivity variance and equipment delay.

Behind reinforced glass, a clean robotic arm places Lucia’s white bag in a
slot, three inches away.

> **ELIAS:** She needs it tonight. When she can’t breathe, she gets scared. And
> being scared makes it worse.  
> **KIOSK:** Command not recognized.

The billing-agent wait is forty-seven minutes. The transaction resets and the
arm returns the medicine to holding.

Another message says 4A is back at 100% efficiency. Its active training profile
is still Elias Morales. They kept the part of him that knew how to be gentle.

## Chapter 5: His Own Ghost

At the back of the notebook, Elias finds a utility map and returns to RGB. In
the server room, the mainframe confirms:

> TRAINING MODEL: SORT_ARM_4A  
> HUMAN CALIBRATION SOURCE: ELIAS MORALES

He tries to delete his profile. ADMIN LOCK. ACCESS DENIED.

> They will not even let him take his own ghost back.

The player chooses to leave it, expose it, or sever the data trunk. If severed,
the electrical arc reaches a carelessly staged pallet of lithium batteries and
dry packaging. The fire is immediate.

## Chapter 6: Recenter

Sector 4 locks down. Elias pulls the manual alarm; the system tells him to
wait. A rack collapses and pins him. Lucia’s drawing lands just beyond his
hand.

4A emerges through smoke. Evacuation protocol orders it to dock, but it breaks
its path. It grips too close and presses the steel harder into Elias.

LOAD INSTABILITY.

Elias reaches the chassis.

> Tap. Tap.

4A releases, shifts two inches, finds the center of gravity, and lifts. Elias
escapes. The hydraulics fail and the rack destroys the machine.

In the desert, the burning facility introduces the story’s first violent
amber. Elias holds the scorched drawing while Lucia’s message plays. He is not
triumphant or redeemed. He is alive, and tomorrow he still has to find the
purple one.
