## 1. Narrative Review: Closing the Core Unsolved Mysteries

While Pass 1 established the brutal living conditions of the three human survivor factions (Meridian, Tallow, Vesper) and Mayor Tina's infiltration, deep inspection of the narrative logs (`LORE_LOGS`, `docs/design/arching-storyline-and-endings.md`, and `docs/lore-coherence-and-secret-sauce-review.md`) reveals **seven major lore threads** that previously lacked physical manifestations:

### Thread 1: Specimen 0047, Bay C, and Director Chen's Sealed Confession
- **The Mystery**: Log C05 documents Pod 312 in Bay C "opened from the inside," while Director Chen's sealed black box contains the terrifying directive: *"You are the containment."* The Crawlers were discovered desperately assembling an antenna before they lacked hands.
- **Physical Manifestations**:
  1. `bay_c_pod_312_rupture`: Specimen 0047's ruptured stasis pod in Bay C, blown open from the inside with outward hydraulic tears and claw gouges.
  2. `crawler_signal_antenna_rig`: The frantic, crude radio antenna the crawlers cobbled together from conduit pipes and severed human nerves.
  3. `chen_sealed_confession_console`: Director Chen's barricaded isolation desk with a hazard-taped black-box flight recorder and a spent service pistol.

### Thread 2: The Three Ships & Class Payloads (Log B03)
- **The Mystery**: *"Three ships. One carries the tracking signal. One carries the relay. One carries the weapon."* The three player classes correspond to these three ships.
- **Physical Manifestations**:
  4. `scout_tracking_beacon_core`: The crashed Scout hull transponder array (explaining why the entity was listening to you).
  5. `engineer_wave_relay_dynamo`: The crashed Engineer hull's ruined sub-core wave relay coil.
  6. `tank_orbital_siege_cannon`: The crashed Tank hull's cracked kinetic tungsten-penetrator cannon pod.

### Thread 3: The Three Alien Hives & Leader Altars (Suture, Relay, Carapace)
- **The Mystery**: The human camps have leaders (Kaelen, Martha, Briggs); the alien hives (Nahl, Vey, Rhun) have full dialogue trees and quest flags (`host_mercy`, `false_clearance`, `guard_oath`), but previously had no unique architectural leader altars.
- **Physical Manifestations**:
  7. `suture_mercy_cocoon_canopy`: Hive Suture's (Nahl) symbiotic healing pod, where humans are preserved rather than digested.
  8. `relay_false_beacon_spire`: Hive Relay's (Vey) radio-assimilated transmitter mimicking corporate rescue distress calls.
  9. `carapace_sentry_chrysalis`: Hive Carapace's (Rhun) heavy armored sentry shield-chrysalis.

### Thread 4: Dr. Okonkwo-Vass & The Communication Outpost
- **The Mystery**: Dr. Vass famously pleads: *"DON'T KILL ONE. TALK TO ONE. PROVE ME RIGHT."* Her research represents the intellectual linchpin of the alien exodus ending.
- **Physical Manifestations**:
  10. `vass_acoustic_resonator_tank`: The submerged acoustic communication tank where Dr. Vass matched human and alien neural frequencies.
  11. `vass_hybrid_embryo_incubator`: Borosilicate glass stasis cylinder displaying a suspended glowing hybrid embryo.

### Thread 5: Sector Zero & The Deep Breach
- **The Mystery**: Horizon dug too deep under Sector Zero, breaking into the primordial hive and waking the Queen.
- **Physical Manifestations**:
  12. `sector_zero_bore_drillhead`: The massive industrial diamond-toothed drill head that broke through into the Queen's vault.
  13. `queen_royal_brood_throne`: The Queen's bedrock plinth weeping glowing golden royal jelly.
  14. `royal_egg_clutch_caul`: Three colossal royal alien eggs wrapped in veined translucent amniotic cauls.

### Thread 6: The Four-Seat Evacuation Shuttle (The Spine of the Endgame)
- **The Mystery**: `ACT2_MANIFEST_SEATS_MAX = 4`. Four seats leave the ice; the Queen takes 2, an egg takes 1, each human/alien leader takes 1.
- **Physical Manifestations**:
  15. `evac_four_seat_cockpit_module`: The four-couch orbital cockpit chassis with hydraulic acceleration harnesses.
  16. `evac_manifest_allocation_terminal`: The flight computer pedestal and passenger manifest scanner deciding who boards.
  17. `evac_preflight_fuel_umbilical`: The heavy cryogenic hypergolic fueling arm and decouple valve.

### Thread 7: Corporate Retribution & Suit Contagion
- **The Mystery**: The Exosuit OS slowly decaying as the player's infection rises, while the worker indentured collars remind them of their debt.
- **Physical Manifestations**:
  18. `decon_exosuit_scrubber_station`: A ruptured suit decontamination airlock shower with chemical nozzles and bio-filter cartridges.
  19. `corp_biometric_collar_chute`: An industrial metal hopper overflowing with severed explosive biometric worker shock collars.

## 2. Production Rules: Studio Neutral Grey Background Standard

To prevent green or magenta chromatic bounce, colored rim highlights, or texture fringing from baking into the 3D meshes during reconstruction, **all 19 GLB asset prompts are authored on a flat, neutral 50% studio mid-grey (`#808080`) background**:
- **Background**: Solid flat pure neutral 18% studio mid-grey (`#808080`) filling the entire frame edge to edge, completely uniform, no gradient, no floor, no horizon, no shadow on the background.
- **Illumination**: Soft, even, 360-degree studio ambient lighting with neutral white kelvin temperature (5500K).
- **Accents**: Exactly one authored single-color LED / bioluminescent accent light per object.
