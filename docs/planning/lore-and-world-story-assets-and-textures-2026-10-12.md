## 1. Executive Summary & Narrative Context

Hunker Bunker is an adult, mature psychological and biomechanical body-horror survival game set on the frozen nightmare world of **Cocytus IV ("The Icecave")**. While previous asset passes established the functional mechanics of the bunker (warehouse shelving, transformers, conveyors, water cisterns, basic sinks), **the world was critically missing explicit placesetters and physical evidence that tell the grim story of what actually happened here.**

Walking through the corridors, three massive narrative voids previously went unrepresented:
1. **Corporate Horizon Atrocities & Indentured Subjugation**: The Horizon Corporation did not just mine ice; they extracted biometric debt, conducted forced cranial extractions, ran disciplinary execution chambers, and processed dead workers into nutrient slurry. The environment lacked the physical instruments of corporate violence: biometric liquidation kiosks, interrogation chairs with cranial drills, and industrial incinerators with scorched human remains.
2. **The Descent of the Three Survivor Factions**:
   - **Meridian (Tech-Scavengers / Overseer Kaelen)**: Descended into cyber-deity fanaticism, soldering human optic nerves and severed spinal columns into machine altars to appease the mainframe.
   - **Tallow (Hydro-Cultists / Sister Martha)**: Pacifists who crossed into horrifying bio-spiritual symbiosis, composting fallen comrades into geothermal fermenters to nurse mutated fetal hybrids in spore cradles.
   - **Vesper (The Iron Guild / Commander Briggs)**: Brutal military pragmatists holding the line with improvised heavy autocannon bipods, frontline field amputation tables heaped with severed limbs, and disciplinary hanging gibbet cages.
3. **Mayor Tina's Secret Infiltration & PregAlien Hive Assimilation**:
   - Tina is an infiltrator whose secret encounter is live in code, but the world lacked her forensic trace: her discarded, molted human dermis husk, her covert footlocker nursery transmitting human distress calls through severed vocal cords, and her dissection vanity.
   - The Hive itself needed to shed sanitization: explicit, sinuous Giger biomechanical sensuality, visceral ovipositors crowning with embryonic sacs, crew members vivisected and wired into ventilation bulkheads, and pulsating reproductive sphincters.

This document authors **40 new mature in-game 3D object prompts** and **20 visceral decals and textures** (12 seamless PBR floor/wall surfaces and 8 chroma-keyed route strips and impact marks).

## 2. Pipeline & Production Rules

### 2.1 The Image-to-3D Pipeline
1. **Generate the still**: Copy the prompt block verbatim. Each block defines subject, dimensions, lore context, single accent light, mature graphic style, render specs, flat chroma background, and negative exclusion tokens.
2. **Chroma Keying**:
   - **Green Screen (`#00FF00`)**: Used for objects containing red blood, amber lights, raw bone, iron, rust, or neutral metal with zero cyan, green bio-tissue, or bile.
   - **Magenta Screen (`#FF00FF`)**: Used for objects containing green bioluminescence, toxic spores, cyan cryogenic ice, alien resin, or olive tissue to avoid keying destruction.
3. **Image-to-3D Conversion**: Reconstruct keyed RGBA assets into high-detail `.glb` files maintaining up to a 25k triangle ceiling.
4. **Optimization**: Run through `scripts/art/convert_glb_textures_ktx2.py` into Basis Universal KTX2 textures in `public/3d/runtime/new3ds/`.
5. **Registration**: Register in `src/world3dOverlay.js` and assign to dressing kits in `src/roomDressingKits.js`.
